# Release 0.4.0 (2026-09-26) — the open pentest findings fixed

Everything on `master` since 0.3.1 (`v0.3.1`): six fix packages (seven
merges, 82 commits) closing every pentest finding that was still open, plus
the ops changes the box has been waiting for since package 5. What stays open
by design is listed in one place: README, "Known limits".

## 1. What users will notice (read this first)

**Storage moves on first start — and there is no way back.**

- The first start of 0.4.0 moves the **contacts and chats** stores from
  `localStorage` into **IndexedDB** (strict durability). The identity also
  gets a durable copy there (its `localStorage` copy stays).
- **Downgrading afterwards is NOT supported.** An older client does not look
  in IndexedDB. It finds only the generation record left in `localStorage` and
  refuses loudly ("your saved contacts … have been DELETED") instead of
  quietly starting over. The data is still in IndexedDB, and 0.4.0 opens it
  again. Users must **not** "start fresh" in the old client.
- **Reload every open tab after the update.** A tab still running 0.3.x that
  saves afterwards is refused ("changed in another tab"). If its copy is
  newer, the next unlock adopts it and says so.
- **OTP needs IndexedDB and Web Locks.** Browsers without them, such as some
  private or incognito modes, refuse OTP with a message. The other modes work
  there. Without IndexedDB, contacts and chats stay in `localStorage` as
  before.

**Modes and prompts:**

- **RSA mode is gone** (four modes: DHKE, AES-256, PQKEM, OTP). A peer still
  on an old client in RSA mode gets ONE transcript line saying the other side
  no longer supports RSA, then the connection closes cleanly.
- **The guest confirms the owner's key too** (DHKE/PQKEM). Before the guest's
  exchange runs, the admission sheet shows the owner's key ("Is this who you
  are expecting?", Continue / Refuse). This happens once per live session. It
  is skipped for a contact verified in person (🟢) whose pin holds exactly
  that key; the transcript then says "approved without asking".
- **Contacts and chats open in one tab.** A second tab offers **"Use here"**.
  Taking over locks the first tab, which says why. An invite link opened
  while the app is open in another tab needs "Use here" too.
- **An identity saved without encryption keys** now asks before new keys are
  made. Cancel changes nothing.

**New messages:**

- **Gap notice:** when the peer's frames skip, the transcript shows one line
  with the count. Frames we dropped ourselves before verification are not
  blamed on the relay.
- **"Vouch not re-checked":** a 🟡 counts for 7 days after the last
  *successful* check. Errors never extend that window, and a vouch from a
  contact you un-verified stops counting at once.
- **Weak-passphrase warnings** for the identity, pad, transfer and AES-256
  passphrases. They warn and never refuse.

**Android (0.4.0, versionCode 5):**

- Every native dialog window has `FLAG_SECURE`, including the passphrase
  prompt and confirms.
- Console output no longer reaches logcat in release builds.
- `taskAffinity=""`.
- SafeBrowsing is off.
- An identity blob older than one the device already used is refused (native
  floor `identity:<hash>`).

## 2. What changed, by package

- **Package 1 — relay availability + mailbox** (`fix/relay-findings`):
  - ML-DSA verification serialised (the SHAKE fallback was not thread-safe).
  - Per-subject buckets are charged after their gates. Register, challenge
    and verify have their own router.
  - The mailbox has a per-host POST ceiling and a per-row storage charge with
    running totals. The byte budget is 256 MiB; eviction takes the newest
    envelope of the heaviest other inbox. The minimum envelope is 8 KiB.
  - COOP + CORP headers; four unused vendored `@noble` modules deleted.
  - **DB schema migration on first start** (mailbox `size`/`charge` columns,
    counter tables), verified in 8 scenarios including a crash mid-migration
    and rollback + upgrade.
- **Package 2 — web client vs a hostile relay** (`fix/client-hostile-relay`):
  - Transcript-line forgery is closed.
  - The log is bounded.
  - Frames from a superseded socket are dropped.
  - Caps on frame, body and batch size.
  - The verification gate is bound to its live connection (a Medium found in
    review: "It matches" could pin the next session's peer).
- **Package 3 — at-rest + Android** (`fix/atrest-android`):
  - OTP derived floor slots are armed and checked.
  - Every native-floor write is checked, with a Kotlin COMMIT_FAILED latch.
  - Inner-keyed gates.
  - Room pins are revoked on Unverify/Remove.
  - The client persists before it displays or transmits, and latches before
    a download.
  - Web Locks, or no OTP.
  - The Android shell items listed above.
- **Package 3b — durable storage** (`fix/durable-storage`): the durability
  HIGH. Chromium's `localStorage` reaches disk up to about a minute after
  `setItem`, so a crash could reopen an OTP pad at a spent offset (a two-time
  pad), and on Android the native floor could end up ahead of the data.
  - OTP progress now heals forward from a sealed IndexedDB record.
  - Contacts and chats move to IndexedDB.
  - The native floor is advanced only after the durable write.
- **Package 4 — owner decisions** (`fix/owner-decisions`):
  - RSA removed.
  - Guest-side approval.
  - One active tab.
  - Identity anti-rollback: the generation is inside the AEAD, the Android
    floor is raised only after the blob is durable, and a keyless blob is
    never silently re-keyed.
  - F-PROTO-004 and F-CRYPTO-005.
- **Package 5 — ops** (`fix/ops-shiplists`), relevant to this deploy:
  - The second-tier systemd sandbox (`systemd-analyze security`: 7.7 EXPOSED
    → 1.1 OK).
  - Caddy's default logger discarded (no client IPs in journald).
  - One ship list for the relay, the deploy, the APK and iOS.
  - Code root-owned on the box.
- **Package 6 — sweep** (`fix/sweep`):
  - The gap notice (F-CRYPTO-001).
  - The 🟡 window (F-PROTO-006).
  - `GET /api/users/{u}/vouches?by=…` (F-PROTO-007, at most 50 names).
  - `frame-src`/`child-src 'none'` on all three shells.
  - Passphrase warnings.
  - README "Known limits".
  - Four OTP two-time-pad paths found on the way (all pre-existing,
    browser-only). They are closed by one invariant: every writer of a pad
    holds its lock and re-checks after the KDF.
- **Version:**
  - `backend/config.py` `VERSION = "0.4.0"`.
  - Android versionCode 5 / versionName 0.4.0.
  - iOS `MARKETING_VERSION` 0.4.0 / build 4. No IPA is published; the next
    `ios-v0.4.0` tag would build one.

**Wire:** unchanged except the additive `?by=`. A 0.3.1 relay ignores it,
and an old client gets the old answer.

## 4. Deploy

**Order:** the relay can go first; there is no wire change besides `?by=`.
Old APKs and old iOS builds keep working against the 0.4.0 relay. Web users
get 0.4.0 on their next load, so tell them to reload every open tab.

```bash
# from a clean checkout of the release (after merge + tag)
git fetch origin && git checkout --detach v0.4.0
bash deploy/deploy-2026-09-26-v0.4.0.sh
```

What the script does (details in the script and in deploy/README.md):

0. **Preflight:** the checkout is 0.4.0 and clean: `VERSION`, `durable.js`,
   no RSA radio, `frame-src 'none'`, `?by=`, the sandboxed unit, Caddy's
   discarded default logger. **Stage:** the new unit and Caddyfile are copied
   into `/root/secure-chat-0.4.0-<stamp>/stage/`. **Backups** (relay
   stopped):
   - `accounts.db`;
   - the live unit;
   - `/etc/caddy/Caddyfile`;
   - a tarball of the code as it ran;
   - the account count.

   The first run's backup directory is also `/root/secure-chat-pre-0.4.0`.
1. **Copy the code** with both shared exclude lists. No `--delete` (see
   deploy/README.md, "What ships: one list").
   - **1b:** removes by name `client/vendor/README.md` and the four `@noble`
     modules deleted in package 1.
   - Lists (does not delete) anything else on the box that 0.4.0 does not
     ship.
2. **Ownership:**
   - backend/, client/ and venv/ become `root:root`;
   - backend/ and client/ are set to `u=rwX,go=rX`;
   - the count of files owned by `securechat` must be 0.
3. **Unit:**
   - `systemd-analyze verify` on the staged file;
   - `install -m 0644 -o root -g root`, then `daemon-reload` and start;
   - wait for `/healthz` 0.4.0 with no restart in between.

   **If the relay does not come up:** the script prints the journal, puts the
   previous unit back and starts the relay again. If even that fails, it
   restores the previous code too. It exits non-zero either way.
4. **Caddy:**
   - `caddy validate` on the staged file BEFORE installing it;
   - validate again in place, then `systemctl reload caddy`;
   - any failure restores the backup and reloads.

   The `/ios/` block goes live and answers 404 until an IPA is published.
5. **Verify** over loopback on the box:
   - healthz 0.4.0;
   - the served `durable.js` and `index.html` match this checkout (sha256);
   - no RSA radio, four modes;
   - CSP has frame/child/worker-src `'none'`;
   - `vendor/README.md` is gone and 404s;
   - `?by=` with a bad name answers 422 and does not echo it; a good list and
     the old form answer 404 for a missing user;
   - accounts before = after;
   - no `SECURE_CHAT_TRUSTED_PROXIES`, `--no-proxy-headers` present,
     loopback only;
   - files owned by `securechat` = 0;
   - the installed unit and Caddyfile are the new ones;
   - `systemd-analyze security` score (want about 1.1 OK);
   - public `https://138-199-144-35.sslip.io/healthz` answers 0.4.0, and
     `/ios/apps.json` answers 404.
6. **By hand:**
   - a login and a sealed message from the phone, and a live room;
   - after that traffic, `journalctl -u caddy --since <reload>` holds no
     client IP.

## 6. Rollback

**Relay only** (the box):

1. Back up the current state first; never overwrite the only copy.
2. Restore the unit and Caddyfile from `/root/secure-chat-pre-0.4.0/`, then
   `systemctl daemon-reload` and `systemctl reload caddy`.
3. Restore the code with either:
   - `git checkout v0.3.1 -- backend client` in a checkout and the script's
     copy and ownership steps; or
   - on the box: `tar -C /opt/secure-chat -xzf
     /root/secure-chat-pre-0.4.0/code.tgz`, then `chown -R root:root
     /opt/secure-chat/backend /opt/secure-chat/client`.
4. `systemctl restart secure-chat`.

- **Keep the DB.** 0.3.1 runs on the migrated schema (the new columns have
  defaults, and the new tables are ignored). A later 0.4.0 start repairs
  sizes and counters: checked on this branch (section 3).
- Restoring `accounts.db` from the backup is only for a damaged DB. It loses
  every account, vouch and queued message since the deploy.

**Clients cannot follow a rollback.** A web client that already ran 0.4.0
has moved its contacts and chats to IndexedDB. The 0.3.1 client a
rolled-back relay serves:

- **refuses to open contacts and chats** ("your saved contacts … have been
  DELETED"). The data is intact in IndexedDB and comes back when 0.4.0 is
  served again. Users must not choose to start over.
- **OTP** under 0.3.1 has the pre-3b durability bug again (a crash right
  after a send can reopen the pad at a spent offset). Pads keep their
  progress, and 0.4.0 later heals forward to the higher offset, but avoid
  OTP until 0.4.0 is back.
- **Identity:** 0.3.1 still unlocks it (the `localStorage` copy stays; the
  added `gen` field is ignored).

The Android and iOS apps carry their own client, so a relay rollback does
not touch them.
